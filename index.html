Connect to Notion

Enter your Notion integration token and the database ID from your Projects & Ledger database. Data stays in your browser — nothing is stored externally.

How to get these:

1. Go to notion.so/my-integrations → New Integration → give it a name → copy the token.

2. Open your Projects & Ledger database in Notion → click Share → Invite your integration.

3. Copy the database ID from the URL — it's the string between the last slash and the question mark.
